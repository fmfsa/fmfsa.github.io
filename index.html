Causality and Machine Learning.

I study how causality and machine learning can help us understand and learn from complex simulations.

About

I'm a PhD candidate at the Technical University of Denmark, supervised by Francisco C. Pereira and Carlos Lima Azevedo. I am currently working on the APEX project in the Machine Learning for Smart Mobility group. I am also an Academic Visitor in the group of Michael Wooldridge and Ani Calinescu at the University of Oxford, working on the Bounded Agents project. My research focuses on learning causal abstractions and applying them to simulation.

Before the PhD I spent three years as an Applied Scientist at Zalando, designing large-scale optimization and machine-learning systems for logistics networks and pricing.

I hold an MSc in Aerospace Engineering from Instituto Superior Técnico, where I specialized in Computational Fluid Mechanics.
